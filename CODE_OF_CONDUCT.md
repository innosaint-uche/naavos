# Code of Conduct

NAAvOS is a human-centred open-source project. Contributors are expected to act professionally, communicate directly and
respectfully, protect private user and tenant data, and make space for people with different backgrounds, abilities, and
levels of technical experience.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of secrets or personal data,
and knowingly presenting unverified capabilities as production-ready.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled
proportionately, with protection against retaliation.
